Common questions

How do I switch between English and Chinese?

Open iOS Settings › Apps › Fit Party › Language. Fit Party follows that setting.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Your workouts, meals and body data live only on your phone. Before switching, go to Settings › Data › Export Backup and save the files. On the new phone, use Settings › Data › Restore from Backup and pick them.

How do I join or create a party?

Open the Party tab. Create a party and share its 6-character invite code, or enter a code a friend sent you. A party has up to 6 people, and you can be in up to 3 parties.

Why can't I poke someone?

You can poke each teammate once a day, and only if they haven't trained yet. Each person gets at most 2 pokes a day, and none after 10 PM.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap them in your party and choose Report or Block. Reports go only to the developer.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Account › Delete Account. Your profile, friends, posts and party spots are deleted from our server, and the data on your phone is cleared.
