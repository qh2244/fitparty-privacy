Fit Party Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Fit Party is a fitness app for training, meal prep and training with friends in a party. This policy explains what data the app keeps, where, and what you can do about it.

Stored only on your phone

Workouts and training plans, meals, menus and shopping lists, body weight and measurements, pets and achievements. These are never uploaded to our server. Deleting the app, or Settings › Data › Clear Data on This Phone, removes them. You can export a backup at any time in Settings › Data.

Stored on our server

The party features need a small amount of data on our server:

  • Account: an account is created automatically the first time you open the app. It has no email address or phone number, only a random account ID.
  • Profile: your nickname, how your character looks, your level and friend code.
  • Friends and parties: who you are friends with, the parties you are in, and the party names.
  • Daily status: whether you trained each day, which workout and for how long, and each week's boss damage and streaks.
  • Messages: posts, high fives and pokes you send, including any short message you write in a poke.
  • Push notifications: if you allow notifications, a device token so we can tell you when a teammate pokes you or high-fives you.

Only your friends and party members can see your profile and daily status. You choose what your party sees in Settings › Party & Privacy › What Your Party Sees.

Not shared unless you turn it on

Your weight and what you eat are never uploaded. If you turn it on in What Your Party Sees, we share only this week's change in your average weight (not your weight itself) and how many of today's planned meals you ate.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to show your party your progress, deliver pokes and high fives, count the weekly boss and party streaks, and keep people safe (blocking and reports). We do not sell data, show ads, or use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database and account system.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications.

They process data only to provide these services to us.

Reporting and blocking

You can block people and report names or messages. Only the developer sees reports, and uses them only to deal with inappropriate content.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Server data is kept while you have an account. Delete it any time in Settings › Account › Delete Account: your profile, friends, posts, messages, daily status and device token are deleted, and you leave every party. Reports you made or that were made about you are kept without your account attached, so we can still act on abuse.

Children

Fit Party is for people 13 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and in the app, along with the date at the top.
